## 2. Direct Application Scheme 1: NFST

### Scheme Overview
A fully funded fellowship for Scheduled Tribe research scholars pursuing regular, full-time M.Phil and Ph.D programmes in recognized Indian Universities, IITs, NITs, and Institutes of National Importance.

### Eligibility Rules
1. **Tribal Identity:** Must belong to a notified Scheduled Tribe (ST) under Article 342.
2. **Academic Merit:** Minimum **55% aggregate marks** in Master’s degree. (M.Phil marks are not considered for minimum qualifying marks).
3. **Admission Status:** Confirmed admission / registration in regular, full-time M.Phil or Ph.D in a UGC-notified or INI institution.
4. **Age Limit:** Maximum **36 years** as on 1st July of the selection year.
5. **Income Ceiling:** **NO family income limit** applies to NFST under official Ministry guidelines.
6. **Exclusivity:** Cannot hold another government/UGC fellowship concurrently.

### Slots Distribution (750 Total)
- **General ST:** 462 slots
- **Female ST Scholars:** 225 slots (30% horizontal reservation)
- **Divyangjan (PWD):** 38 slots (5% reservation)
- **Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTG):** 25 slots

### Financial Allowances
- **Junior Research Fellowship (JRF):** ₹37,000 / month (Years 1 & 2)
- **Senior Research Fellowship (SRF):** ₹42,000 / month (Years 3 onwards upon university assessment)
- **Contingency Allowance:**
  - Humanities & Social Sciences: ₹10,000/yr (JRF) → ₹20,500/yr (SRF)
  - Science, Engineering & Technology: ₹12,000/yr (JRF) → ₹25,000/yr (SRF)
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** Paid as per Central Government norms (8%, 16%, or 27% based on city classification) if hostel accommodation is not provided by the university.
- **Escorts / Reader Allowance:** ₹2,000 / month for Divyangjan / visually impaired scholars.

### Mandatory Documents
1. Passport-size photograph & signature
2. 10th Board Certificate / Marksheet (Proof of DOB)
3. Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate (issued by competent revenue authority)
4. PVTG Certificate (if claiming PVTG slot)
5. Disability / UDID Certificate (if Divyangjan)
6. Master's Degree Final Marksheet & Degree Certificate (showing min. 55%)
7. CGPA to Percentage Conversion Formula (if grade is CGPA)
8. University Admission / Registration / Joining Order

## 3. Direct Application Scheme 2: NOS

### Scheme Overview
Provides financial assistance to meritorious ST students for pursuing Master’s level courses, Ph.D, and Post-Doctoral research in accredited foreign universities ranked within the **Top 1000** in the latest QS World University Rankings.

### Eligibility Rules
1. **Tribal Identity:** Belongs to a notified Scheduled Tribe (ST).
2. **Academic Merit:** Minimum **55% marks** or equivalent grade in qualifying degree:
   - For Master’s: Bachelor’s Degree
   - For Ph.D: Master’s Degree
   - For Post-Doctoral: Master’s Degree + Ph.D awarded
3. **Family Income Limit:** Total family income from all sources must not exceed **₹6,00,000 per annum**. (Orphans supported by guardians are completely exempt).
4. **University Ranking:** Confirmed admission in a foreign university ranked within the **Top 1000** in QS World University Rankings.
5. **Age Limits (as on 1st July):**
   - Master’s: Maximum 32 years
   - Ph.D: Maximum 35 years
   - Post-Doctoral: Maximum 38 years
6. **Family Ceiling:** Not more than two children of the same parents are eligible.
7. **Single Award:** Applicant must not have received this overseas scholarship previously.

### Slots Distribution (20 Total)
- **General ST:** 17 slots
- **PVTG Scholars:** 3 slots

### Financial Allowances
- **Annual Maintenance Allowance:**
  - United States: USD 15,400 / annum
  - United Kingdom: GBP 9,900 / annum
  - Other Countries: Equivalent USD
- **Tuition Fees:** Actual tuition fees paid directly to the foreign university.
- **Contingency Allowance:** USD 1,500 / annum (or GBP 1,100 / annum in UK).
- **Travel:** Economy class airfare to destination country and return flight upon course completion.

### Mandatory Documents
1. Passport-size photograph & signature
2. Class 10 Certificate (Proof of DOB)
3. Valid Passport (Photo & Address pages)
4. Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate
5. Income Certificate (competent revenue authority) or Orphan Certificate
6. Repeatable Family Income Breakdown
7. Qualifying Degree Marksheets & Certificates
8. Unconditional Admission Offer Letter from Foreign University (with QS ranking proof)
9. Employer NOC (if currently employed)
10. Gap in studies declaration / affidavit (if gap exceeds 6 months)

## 4. External Federated Schemes

For the Centrally Sponsored Schemes (**Pre-Matric**, **Post-Matric**) and the Premier Institute Scheme (**Top Class Education**), TribeXcel operates as an authoritative information and advisory portal.

### Top Class Education for ST Students
- **Overview:** 1000 scholarships annually for ST students securing admission in 250+ notified premier institutions (IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, NLUs).
- **Income Limit:** Up to ₹6,00,000 / annum.
- **Application Portal:** National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
- **TribeXcel Role:** Eligibility evaluation, rules advisory, and direct NSP federated redirection.

### Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students
- **Overview:** Centrally sponsored open entitlement scheme for post-secondary education (Class XI through Ph.D).
- **Income Limit:** Up to ₹2,50,000 / annum.
- **Application Portals:** State Scholarship Portals & National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

### Pre-Matric Scholarship for ST Students
- **Overview:** Centrally sponsored scholarship for ST students studying in Classes IX and X in government and recognized schools.
- **Income Limit:** Up to ₹2,50,000 / annum.
- **Application Portals:** State Scholarship Portals & National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
